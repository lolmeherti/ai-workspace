The iPhone 16 Pro features a comprehensive pro camera system with several key components and advanced features [S1-C10].

**Main Camera System:**
*   **Primary Sensor:** 48MP Fusion camera [S1-C10].
    *   **Specifications:** 24 mm, ƒ/1.78 aperture, 100% Focus Pixels, and second-generation sensor-shift optical image stabilization [S1-C10].
    *   **Functionality:** Supports super-high-resolution photos (24MP and 48MP) [S1-C10].
    *   **Zoom/Photography:** It can take optical-quality 2x zoom photos at 12MP, and allows for video recording up to 4K quality at 120fps [S3-C7].
    *   **Zoom Range:** The total optical zoom range is 10x, with 5x optical zoom in and 2x optical zoom out [S1-C10].
    *   **Macro:** It supports 48MP macro photography [S1-C10].

**Telephoto and Ultra-Wide Cameras:**
*   **Ultra Wide Camera:** 48MP [S3-C7].
    *   **Specifications:** 13 mm, ƒ/2.2 aperture, 120° field of view, and Hybrid Focus Pixels [S1-C10, S3-C7].
*   **Telephoto Camera:** 12MP [S3-C7].
    *   **Specifications:** 120 mm, ƒ/2.8 aperture, 20° field of view, 100% Focus Pixels, seven-element lens, and 3D sensor-shift optical image stabilization and autofocus, utilizing a tetraprism design [S1-C10, S3-C7].

**Front Camera:**
*   The front camera is 12MP [S3-C7].
    *   **Specifications:** ƒ/1.9 aperture and 1/3.6-inch sensor [S3-C7].

**Key Features and Controls:**
*   **Zoom:** Digital zoom is available up to 25x [S1-C10].
*   **Physical Controls:** The phone includes a dedicated Camera Control button, which is a hardware key for launching and controlling the camera [S1-C23, S3-C7].
*   **Stabilization and Hardware:** The device includes a LiDAR Scanner [S1-C23, S1-C10].
*   **Software/Imaging Features:** Advanced features include:
    *   Photonic Engine and Deep Fusion [S1-C10].
    *   Smart HDR 5 [S1-C10].
    *   Next-generation portraits with Focus and Depth Control, and six Portrait Lighting effects [S1-C10].
    *   Night mode, which is enabled by the LiDAR Scanner [S1-C10].
    *   Panorama capture (up to 63MP) [S1-C10].
    *   Support for Apple ProRAW, Wide color capture, and Spatial photos [S1-C10].
*   **Durability:** The lens cover is made of Sapphire crystal [S1-C10].
