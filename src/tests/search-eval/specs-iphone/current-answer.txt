The iPhone 16 Pro features a sophisticated camera system consisting of a multi-lens rear setup and a TrueDepth front camera, supported by advanced processing and video capabilities.

### **Rear Pro Camera System**
The rear system includes four distinct lenses and advanced stabilization:

*   **48MP Fusion Camera:** 24 mm, ƒ/1.78 aperture, 1/1.28" sensor, 1.22µm pixels, 2nd-gen sensor-shift OIS, and 100% Focus Pixels. It supports super-high-resolution photos at 24MP and 48MP.
*   **12MP 2x Telephoto:** 48 mm, ƒ/1.78 aperture, 1/3.06" sensor, 1.12µm pixels, 2nd-gen sensor-shift OIS, and 100% Focus Pixels.
*   **48MP Ultra Wide:** 13 mm, ƒ/2.2 aperture, 1/2.55" sensor, 0.7µm pixels, 120° field of view, and Hybrid Focus Pixels. It also supports 48MP super-high-resolution photos.
*   **12MP 5x Telephoto:** 120 mm, ƒ/2.8 aperture, 20° field of view, 100% Focus Pixels, seven-element lens, and a tetraprism design with 3D sensor-shift OIS and autofocus.
*   **Zoom Capabilities:** The system offers a 5x optical zoom in, 2x optical zoom out, a 10x optical zoom range, and up to 25x digital zoom.

### **Video Recording**
The iPhone 16 Pro offers extensive video formats and frame rate options:

*   **Resolution and Frame Rates:**
    *   **4K Dolby Vision:** 24, 25, 30, 60, 100, or 120 fps (via Fusion).
    *   **1080p Dolby Vision:** 25, 30, 60, 120, or 240 fps (via Fusion).
    *   **ProRes:** Up to 4K at 120 fps (requires external recording).
    *   **Slo-mo:** 1080p up to 240 fps; 4K Dolby Vision up to 120 fps (via Fusion).
*   **Specialized Modes:**
    *   **Cinematic Mode:** Up to 4K Dolby Vision at 30 fps.
    *   **Action Mode:** Up to 2.8K Dolby Vision at 60 fps.
    *   **Log Video:** Uses the Academy Color Encoding System.
    *   **Spatial Video:** 1080p at 30 fps.
    *   **Other:** Macro video, Night mode Time-lapse, and QuickTake (up to 4K Dolby Vision at 60 fps).
*   **Audio and Stabilization:** Features include Spatial Audio, stereo recording, four studio-quality mics, wind noise reduction, Audio Mix, and audio zoom. Stabilization is provided by 2nd-gen sensor-shift (Fusion) and 3D sensor-shift (Telephoto).

### **Key Features and Processing**
*   **Hardware:** Includes a Camera Control button, Sapphire crystal lens cover, Adaptive True Tone flash, and a LiDAR Scanner (TOF 3D).
*   **Processing & Software:** Utilizes the Photonic Engine, Deep Fusion, Smart HDR 5, and Apple ProRAW.
*   **Photography Modes:** Next-generation portraits (with Focus and Depth Control), Portrait Lighting (6 effects), Night mode (with LiDAR), Panorama (up to 63MP), Photographic Styles, Spatial photos, and 48MP macro photography.
*   **Supported Formats:** HEIF, JPEG, and DNG.

### **TrueDepth Front Camera**
*   **Specifications:** 12MP, 23 mm (wide), ƒ/1.9 aperture, 1/3.6" sensor, 1.0µm pixels, Autofocus with Focus Pixels, OIS SL 3D (depth/biometrics sensor), and Retina Flash.
*   **Video Capabilities:** 4K Dolby Vision (up to 60 fps), 1080p (up to 120 fps), Cinematic mode (up to 4K at 30 fps), and ProRes (up to 4K at 60 fps with external recording).
*   **Features:** Photonic Engine, Deep Fusion, Smart HDR 5, Night mode, Apple ProRAW, and Spatial Audio.
