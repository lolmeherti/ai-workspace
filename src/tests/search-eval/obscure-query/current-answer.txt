Because the "modern" design of the retractable tape measure evolved through several key improvements, the answer depends on which specific feature is being considered:

* **The design resembling the common handyman's tool:** **Alvin J. Fellows** is credited with creating the first design that closely resembles the common modern tool. He patented his improved version on **July 14, 1868**. His design improved upon earlier American models by adding a locking mechanism (which holds the tape in place until a button is pressed) and an all-in-one compact metal case.
* **The basis for most modern pocket tape measures:** **Hiram A. Farrand** patented the concave-convex tape on **January 3, 1922**. This specific design allowed the metal tape to remain rigid when extended and serves as the foundation for most modern pocket tape measures used today.

For historical context, the very first retractable tape measure (featuring a spring-loaded cloth strip) was invented by British metalworker **James Chesterman** in **1821**.
