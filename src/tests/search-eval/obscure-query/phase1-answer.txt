The history of the retractable tape measure involves several key inventors and designs:

*   **First Retractable Tape Measure:** James Chesterman, a British metalworker, is credited with inventing the first retractable tape measure in 1821 [S1]. His original design featured a spring-loaded cloth strip with markings, housed in a compact case [S1].
*   **Early Patents:** William H. Bangs, a Connecticut resident, obtained a patent for the first design of a spring return tape measure in 1864 [S1, S2].
*   **Design Basis for Modern Pocket Tapes:** The concave/convex tape design developed by H. A. Farrand became the basis for most modern pocket tape measures, a design that was slowly adopted by carpenters in the late 1920s [S1].
*   **Modernized Retractable Design:** Alvin J. Fellows received a patent on July 14, 1868, for a unique spring-loaded, locking tape measure design [S1, S2]. Fellows improved upon Bangs’ design by adding a locking mechanism to hold the tape in place and by enclosing the entire measure in a compact metal case. This design is noted as being the first to resemble the common handyman’s tool used today [S2].
