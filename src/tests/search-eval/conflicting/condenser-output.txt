**Study Focus:** A systematic review and meta-analysis of randomized controlled trials (RCTs) regarding dark chocolate consumption and cardiovascular health (Source: PMC9589144).

**Health Benefits:**
* **Blood Pressure:** Significant reduction in both systolic and diastolic blood pressure.
* **Lipid Profile:** Improvements in cholesterol levels, specifically increasing HDL ("good" cholesterol) and potentially decreasing LDL ("bad" cholesterol).
* **Endothelial Function:** Enhanced vascular function (the ability of blood vessels to dilate).

**Risks and Considerations:**
* **Nutritional Composition:** The health benefits are primarily driven by cocoa flavanols; many commercial dark chocolates contain high levels of sugar and saturated fats, which can offset benefits.
* **Caloric Intake:** Excessive consumption can lead to weight gain due to high energy density.
* **Dosage:** The study emphasizes that the concentration of flavanols is key to therapeutic effects.
