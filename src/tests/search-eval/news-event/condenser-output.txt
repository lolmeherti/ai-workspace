### **Event Overview**
* **Date:** July 19, 2024
* **Cause:** A faulty configuration update distributed by CrowdStrike for its **Falcon Sensor** security software. The update was intended to improve the evaluation of **named pipe execution** on Microsoft Windows.
* **Technical Detail:** 
    * The update runs at the **kernel level** (the core of the operating system), which amplified the impact of the error.
    * A modification to "Channel File 291" caused an out-of-bounds memory read, resulting in an invalid page fault on Windows systems.
    * **Version Specificity:** The flaw was present in versions with the timestamp **2024-07-19 0409 UTC**. Versions timestamped **2024-07-19 0527 UTC** or later did not contain the bug.
* **Root Cause Analysis:** CrowdStrike subsequently published a full Root Cause Analysis (RCA) report.
* **Primary Symptom:** Microsoft Windows "Blue Screen of Death" (BSOD), bootloops, or systems entering recovery mode.

### **Scope and Impact**
* **Affected Systems:** 
    * Initial reports cited approximately **8.5 million** Microsoft Windows operating systems (primarily Windows 10 and 11). 
    * **Note on Scale:** Microsoft later clarified that the 8.5 million figure represented only those who shared crash reports; the actual number of impacted devices is believed to be significantly higher.
* **Unaffected Systems:** macOS, Linux, and most personal Windows PCs (the software is primarily used by large organizations).
* **Global Disruption:** Affected critical services including airlines, banks, hospitals, manufacturing, stock markets, and government services.
    * **Aviation:** Delta Air Lines was severely impacted, with over 37,000 computers affected, disrupting 1.3 million passengers and resulting in over $500 million in losses.
    * **Healthcare:** North American hospitals paused non-urgent visits, and the UK’s National Health Service (NHS) experienced paralysis in GP practices due to an inability to access medical records/prescriptions.
* **Financial Impact:** 
    * **Global:** Estimated in the tens of billions of dollars.
    * **CrowdStrike Specific:** Estimated losses of $5.4 billion; the company's share price dropped by almost 25%, devaluing the company by more than $20 billion.
* **Cyberattack Status:** CrowdStrike confirmed the outage was **not** the result of a cyberattack.
* **Secondary Threats (Exploitation):** 
    * **Phishing & Brand Impersonation:** Threat actors used the chaos to send malicious emails and use domains impersonating CrowdStrike.
    * **Malicious Files:** Distribution of malicious ZIP archives, specifically targeting customers in Latin America.
    * **Handala Hacking Team:** Approximately two weeks after the outage, this group exploited the situation by posing as CrowdStrike support via phone calls and phishing emails.

### **Remediation**
* **CrowdStrike Action:** The company reverted the faulty content update within hours and provided instructional videos/updated processes to accelerate recovery.
* **Microsoft Tools:** Microsoft released a recovery tool that uses a USB drive to boot and repair affected systems.
* **Manual Fix Required:** Because many systems were stuck in bootloops, technical staff often had to manually intervene by:
    * Booting into Safe Mode or the Windows Recovery Environment.
    * Deleting specific `.sys` files (starting with `C-00000291-`) located in the `%windir%\System32\drivers\CrowdStrike\` directory.
* **Cloud Environments:** Specific guidance was issued for remediation in cloud environments, such as **Azure** and **AWS**.
* **Complications:** Systems with **BitLocker** encryption required manual entry of 48-digit recovery keys, which was exacerbated when recovery keys were stored on servers that had also crashed.

### **Related Vulnerabilities**
* **CVE-2024-6768:** A vulnerability discovered in the Windows **Common Log File System (CLFS)** driver affecting Windows 10 and 11. 
    * **Impact:** Allows a minimally authenticated attacker to perform a denial-of-service (DoS) attack, potentially resulting in a BSOD similar to the CrowdStrike outage. 
    * **Status:** A Proof of Concept (PoC) exists, but at the time of the report, no patch was available from Microsoft.
