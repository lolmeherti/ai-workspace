The system requirements for Adobe Photoshop can be viewed in two ways: the official minimum and recommended specifications for running the software, and independent hardware recommendations for optimal performance.

### Official Technical Requirements (Version 27.x and Later) [S1]

### Performance and Hardware Recommendations [S2, S3]

For optimal performance, ProX PC provides its own hardware benchmarks for Photoshop [S2-C4].

**Processor (CPU) [S2-C4, S3-C8]:**
*   The CPU is a critical component of a Photoshop workstation [S2-C4].
*   Currently, Intel’s Core i9 14900K is considered the best CPU for Photoshop, leading the 13th & 14th Gen Core and AMD Ryzen 7000 Series processors in their benchmark [S2-C4].
*   The i7 14700K is noted as a solid, more budget-friendly alternative [S2-C4].
*   While Adobe has improved core utilization, having more than approximately 8 cores generally only results in a minimal performance increase, and the CPU architecture is often more important than the raw core count [S2-C4].
*   Currently, Intel has a slight advantage over AMD [S2-C4].
*   It is advised not to aim for a Threadripper or Xeon CPU, as these will not offer additional improvement when running Adobe Photoshop [S3-C8].

**Memory (RAM) [S3-C8]:**
*   When selecting RAM, priority should be given to quantity over quality [S3-C8].
*   The bare minimum quantity for RAM is 8 GB, but upgrading to 16 GB is recommended for a significant performance boost [S3-C8].
*   It is important to plan for future upgrades by choosing two sticks of memory instead of more sticks of smaller capacity [S3-C8].

**General Notes [S2-C4]:**
*   ProX PC runs extensive hardware benchmarks to ensure that the systems they sell are tailored for Photoshop, as official system requirements lists can sometimes be outdated or sub-optimal [S2-C4].
