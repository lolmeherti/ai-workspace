### Adobe Photoshop 2025 System Requirements

#### **Windows**
* **Processor:** Multicore Intel® or AMD processor (2 GHz or faster) with SSE 4.2 or later and 64-bit support.
* **Operating System:** Windows 10 64-bit (version 21H2 or later); LTSC versions are not supported.
* **RAM:** 
    * **Minimum:** 8 GB
    * **Recommended:** 16 GB or more
* **Graphics (GPU):** 
    * **Minimum:** GPU with DirectX 12 (feature level 12_0 or later) and 1.5 GB GPU memory.
    * **Recommended:** GPU with DirectX 12 support and 4 GB GPU memory (for 4K displays or greater). 
    * *Note: Prefer GPUs less than 7 years old and keep drivers updated.*
* **Monitor Resolution:** 
    * **Minimum:** 1280 x 800 at 100% UI scaling.
    * **Recommended:** 1920 x 1080 or greater at 100% UI scaling.
* **Storage:** 
    * **Minimum:** 20 GB of hard disk space.
    * **Recommended:** 100 GB on a fast internal SSD. A separate internal drive is recommended for scratch disks.
* **Internet Connection:** Required for software activation, subscription validation, and online services.

#### **macOS (Intel-based)**
* **Processor:** Multicore Intel® (2 GHz or faster) with SSE 4.2 or later and 64-bit support.
* **Operating System:** 
    * **Minimum:** macOS Big Sur (version 11.0) or later.
    * **Recommended:** macOS Ventura (version 13.5.1).
    * *Note: Installation is blocked on macOS v10.15.x. Case-sensitive file systems are not supported.*
* **RAM:** 
    * **Minimum:** 8 GB
    * **Recommended:** 16 GB or more
* **Graphics (GPU):** 
    * **Minimum:** GPU with Metal support and 1.5 GB memory.
    * **Recommended:** GPU with Metal support and 4 GB memory (for 4K displays).
* **Display:** 
    * **Minimum:** 1280 x 800 at 100% UI scaling.
    * **Recommended:** 1920 x 1080 or greater at 100% UI scaling.
* **Storage:** 
    * **Minimum:** 20 GB of hard disk space.
    * **Recommended:** 100 GB on a fast internal SSD. High-speed drives are suggested for scratch disks.

#### **macOS (Apple Silicon)**
* **Processor:** ARM-based Apple Silicon.
* **Operating System:** 
    * **Minimum:** macOS Big Sur (version 11.2.2) or later.
    * **Recommended:** macOS Sonoma (version 14.4.1) or later.
* **RAM, Graphics, & Storage:** Same requirements as Intel-based macOS systems.

### **Performance Recommendations & Professional Tips**
* **CPU:** 
    * Architecture is often more important than raw core count. While Adobe is improving multi-core support, having more than 8 cores provides diminishing returns for most tasks.
    * **Brand Parity:** Performance differences between modern AMD and Intel processors are often unnoticeable; choose based on price-to-performance value.
* **GPU:** 
    * While GPU acceleration is expanding, the demand remains relatively light. A mid-range card (e.g., NVIDIA RTX 4070) performs nearly as well as high-end cards (e.g., RTX 4090) for most tasks. NVIDIA is generally preferred over AMD for driver reliability and performance.
* **RAM:** 
    * **Prioritize Quantity:** Aim for capacity (GB) over speed (MHz) or low latency. 
    * **Scaling:** 
        * **16 GB:** Recommended minimum for general use.
        * **32 GB:** Recommended for documents between 500 MB and 1 GB.
        * **64 GB+:** Recommended for very large/complex documents.
    * **Upgrade Strategy:** When building a system, use fewer, higher-capacity sticks (e.g., 2 x 16GB) rather than many small sticks (e.g., 4 x 8GB) to leave motherboard slots open for future upgrades.
* **Storage & Scratch Disks:** 
    * Use a fast internal SSD for both the software and as a scratch disk.
    * **Maintenance:** Keep at least 20 GB of free space on your SSD to prevent performance degradation.
* **Hardware Choice (Laptop vs. Desktop):** 
    * **Desktop:** Generally offers better value, more raw power, and superior cooling compared to laptops at the same price point.
    * **Laptop:** If mobility is required, Apple Silicon (M-series) MacBooks are highly recommended for their performance efficiency.
