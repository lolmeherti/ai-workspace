The system requirements for Adobe Photoshop 2025 vary depending on whether you are using Windows or macOS. Below is a detailed breakdown of the requirements and professional performance recommendations.

### **Windows Requirements**
* **Processor:** Multicore Intel® or AMD processor (2 GHz or faster) with 64-bit support and SSE 4.2 or later.
* **Operating System:** Windows 10 64-bit (version 21H2 or later). Note that LTSC versions are not supported.
* **RAM:** 
    * **Minimum:** 8 GB
    * **Recommended:** 16 GB or more
* **Graphics (GPU):** 
    * **Minimum:** GPU with DirectX 12 (feature level 12_0 or later) and 1.5 GB GPU memory.
    * **Recommended:** GPU with DirectX 12 support and 4 GB GPU memory (especially for 4K displays). It is recommended to use GPUs less than 7 years old and keep drivers updated.
* **Monitor Resolution:** 
    * **Minimum:** 1280 x 800 at 100% UI scaling.
    * **Recommended:** 1920 x 1080 or greater at 100% UI scaling.
* **Storage:** 
    * **Minimum:** 20 GB of hard disk space.
    * **Recommended:** 100 GB on a fast internal SSD. A separate internal drive is recommended for scratch disks.
* **Internet Connection:** Required for software activation, subscription validation, and online services.

---

### **macOS Requirements**
Requirements for macOS are divided between older Intel-based Macs and newer Apple Silicon models.

#### **Intel-based Macs**
* **Processor:** Multicore Intel® (2 GHz or faster) with 64-bit support and SSE 4.2 or later.
* **Operating System:** 
    * **Minimum:** macOS Big Sur (version 11.0) or later.
    * **Recommended:** macOS Ventura (version 13.5.1).
    * *Note: Installation is blocked on macOS v10.15.x, and case-sensitive file systems are not supported.*
* **RAM:** 8 GB minimum; 16 GB or more recommended.
* **Graphics (GPU):** GPU with Metal support and 1.5 GB memory minimum; 4 GB memory recommended for 4K displays.
* **Display:** 1280 x 800 minimum; 1920 x 1080 or greater recommended.
* **Storage:** 20 GB minimum; 100 GB on a fast internal SSD recommended.

#### **Apple Silicon Macs**
* **Processor:** ARM-based Apple Silicon.
* **Operating System:** 
    * **Minimum:** macOS Big Sur (version 11.2.2) or later.
    * **Recommended:** macOS Sonoma (version 14.4.1) or later.
* **RAM, Graphics, & Storage:** These follow the same requirements as the Intel-based macOS systems listed above.

---

### **Professional Performance Recommendations**
To get the most out of Photoshop 2025, consider these hardware optimization tips:

* **CPU:** Architecture is often more important than core count. While multi-core support is improving, having more than 8 cores typically provides diminishing returns. You can choose between AMD and Intel based on price-to-performance, as modern differences are often unnoticeable.
* **GPU:** While GPU acceleration is growing, demand remains relatively light. A mid-range card (like an NVIDIA RTX 4070) is often sufficient. NVIDIA is generally preferred over AMD due to better driver reliability.
* **RAM:** Prioritize capacity (GB) over speed (MHz). 
    * **16 GB:** Recommended for general use.
    * **32 GB:** Recommended for documents between 500 MB and 1 GB.
    * **64 GB+:** Recommended for very large or complex documents.
    * *Tip: Use fewer, higher-capacity sticks (e.g., 2 x 16GB) to leave motherboard slots open for future upgrades.*
* **Storage:** Use a fast internal SSD for both the software and your scratch disk. Ensure you keep at least 20 GB of free space on your SSD to prevent performance drops.
* **Hardware Choice:** Desktops offer better value, power, and cooling. If you require mobility, Apple Silicon (M-series) MacBooks are highly recommended for their efficiency.
